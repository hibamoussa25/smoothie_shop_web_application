Berry Bliss Smoothies is a family-owned company located in Arlington, TX. We make fresh and delicious smoothies using locally sourced fruit. Our mission is to reduce food waste while offering healthy and flavorful drinks. The company partners with local farms and uses eco-friendly packaging to support the planet. Each smoothie is made with care, combining nutrition, freshness, and creativity. Berry Bliss believes that doing good for the environment can taste good too.

A top-rated DFW favorite known for our fresh, flavorful smoothies!
