Berry Bliss Smoothies is a family-owned business in Arlington, TX, founded in 2018 from a passion for fresh fruit and drink making. Our company began by partnering with local farms to turn high quality, locally sourced produce into delicious smoothies. Over the years, Berry Bliss has grown into a trusted community favorite known for our focus on freshness, creativity, and eco-friendly practices.


Our mission at Berry Bliss Smoothies is to offer healthy, flavorful drinks while reducing food waste and supporting the environment. We use locally sourced fruit, craft each smoothie with care, and choose eco-friendly packaging to help protect the planet. We believe that making responsible choices can taste great and make a positive impact.

Who is Behind Berry Bliss?

Elena Brooks founded Berry Bliss Smoothies in 2018 with the goal of creating fresh, flavorful drinks for her community. Her background in business and love for locally sourced ingredients continue to guide the company’s eco-friendly approach.
